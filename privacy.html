Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

HEC TECH helps you organize a personal collection of tools and related manuals. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Tool details you enter or edit, including names, models, notes, dates, locations, and photos, are stored in your browser on your device. Offline PDF manuals you attach are stored on that device. HEC TECH does not send this inventory to its owner.

Google Drive backup

If you connect Google Drive, HEC TECH uses Google sign-in to back up or restore your inventory and attached offline PDF manuals. Backup files are stored in the private app-data area of your Google Drive account. The app may attempt a backup when it opens if you have already authorized Drive; you can also use the backup controls in the app.

Google handles sign-in. HEC TECH does not ask for or receive your Google password. The app uses its Cloudflare Worker service to complete authorization and relay the Drive requests needed for backup and restore. Google account data is used only to provide these features, not for advertising, sale, or unrelated purposes. HEC TECH's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting or revoking Google access stops future access but does not automatically remove a backup already stored in your Drive account. Removing app data from your device also does not remove a Drive backup.

External links

HEC TECH may open external product, accessory, or manual links, including retailer websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices; review their policies when you visit them.

Sharing and tracking

HEC TECH does not sell your tool inventory or use it for advertising. The app does not include advertising or analytics trackers. Google, Cloudflare, GitHub Pages, and external sites may process technical information as part of providing their services and protecting their systems under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove tool records in the app.
  • Clear the app's browser storage to remove the local inventory and offline PDFs from that device. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.
  • Manage or revoke HEC TECH's Google access in your Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete an existing Drive backup.

Children

HEC TECH is a general-purpose personal tool organizer and is not designed for children under 13.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if HEC TECH's features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact hectorkidd@gmail.com.
