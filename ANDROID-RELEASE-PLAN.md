# HEC TECH Android release plan

Keep the existing web app as the shared feature codebase. Finish Pro before recruiting the 12 testers. The 12-tester process targets Google Play (Android); Apple App Store requires a separate release process.

## Implemented preview
Saved rooms and openings; project plans; paint, drywall sheet, flooring box estimates; suggested inventory tool matches; retailer searches; project shopping lists and purchased checkmarks; complete backup support.

## Before paid launch
- Fastener, joint tape and compound estimates based on explicit product/installation assumptions.
- Improve tool matching with user confirmation and flooring tools.
- Printable/shareable project summaries and editable shopping entries.
- Android TWA package, stable origin and Digital Asset Links, signing and Android App Bundle.
- Server-verified Pro entitlements, Google Play subscription integration, purchase recovery and cancellation handling.
- Authenticated server-side lookup quotas, usage reporting and shared search budget controls.
- Validate camera, barcode, photos, PDFs, backups, offline behavior, accessibility and reinstall recovery on Android devices.
- Store listing, privacy/data safety disclosures and final policy review.
- Closed testing with eligible testers; apply for production access after requirements are met.

Planned pricing: Free inventory; Pro $4.99 monthly or $39.99 annually. No billing currently active. Affiliate approval is separate; current links are ordinary retailer searches.
